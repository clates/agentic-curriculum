# See how a child is doing and print an old week

This guide shows you how to open a child's page in the hosted app, print a past week again, and
read the per-subject progress bars.

## Open the child's page

1. Open "This week".
2. On the child's card, press "Past weeks" or "Skills by subject". Both open the child's page, at
   that section.

To go back, press "Back to this week".

## Print a past week again

1. On the child's page, find the week in "Past weeks". The newest is first.
2. Press "{Name}'s pages", "Teacher guide" or "Prep sheet". Each opens in a new tab.
3. Print from that tab.

A week has only the documents that exist. An older week may have just the child's pages. Under each
week's dates, "Checked in" or "Not checked in yet" says whether you've done that week's check-in.

## Read "Skills by subject"

Each subject has a bar with counts of solid, shaky and still-to-come skills: [what each count
means](../reference/children.md#get-studentsstudent_idcoverage).

## If something goes wrong

- **"We couldn't find that child."** The link is for a child that doesn't exist or isn't in your
  family. Go back to "This week".
- **"No weeks yet."** No week has been written for the child.
- **"Nothing to show yet."** No skills are listed for the child's grades and nothing has been
  checked in. A new child normally sees bars that are all "still to come" instead.
- **An old week is missing.** The page shows the newest 50 weeks only.

Fields and rules: [Children reference](../reference/children.md#child-page).
