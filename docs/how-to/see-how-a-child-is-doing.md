# See how a child is doing and print an old week

This guide shows you how to open a child's page in the hosted app, print a past week again, and
read the per-subject progress bars.

## Open the child's page

1. Open "This week".
2. On the child's card, press "Past weeks" or "How it's going". Both open the child's page, at that
   section.

To go back, press "Back to this week".

## Print a past week again

1. On the child's page, find the week in "Past weeks". The newest is first.
2. Press "{Name}'s pages", "Teacher guide" or "Prep sheet". Each opens in a new tab.
3. Print from that tab.

A week has only the documents that exist. An older week may have just the child's pages.

## Read "How it's going"

Each subject has a bar and three counts:

- **solid**: skills the child has mastered.
- **shaky**: skills the child is still working on.
- **still to come**: skills at the child's grade that haven't started.

The page shows counts only, not skill names. "Nothing to show yet" means no week has been checked
in.

## If something goes wrong

- **"We couldn't find that child."** The link is for a child that doesn't exist or isn't in your
  family. Go back to "This week".
- **"No weeks yet."** No week has been written for the child.
- **An old week is missing.** The page shows the newest 50 weeks only.

Fields and rules: [Children reference](../reference/children.md#child-page). The definitions of
solid and shaky: [coverage endpoint](../reference/children.md#get-studentsstudent_idcoverage).
