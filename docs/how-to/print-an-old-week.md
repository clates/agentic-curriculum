# Print an old week

This guide shows you how to print a week you've already been given, again.

1. Open "This week".
2. On the child's card, press "Past weeks". The child's page opens at that section.
3. Find the week. The newest is first.
4. Press "{Name}'s pages", "Teacher guide" or "Prep sheet". Each opens in a new tab.
5. Print from that tab.

A week has only the documents that exist. An older week may have just the child's pages. Under each
week's dates, "Checked in" or "Not checked in yet" says whether you've done that week's check-in.

## If something goes wrong

- **"We couldn't find that child."** The link is for a child that doesn't exist or isn't in your
  family. Go back to "This week".
- **"No weeks yet."** No week has been written for the child.
- **"We couldn't load the documents for this week."** Press "Try again" under that week.
- **An old week is missing.** The page shows the newest 50 weeks only.

To go back, press "Back to this week". Fields and rules, including the "Skills by subject" counts:
[Children reference](../reference/children.md#child-page).
