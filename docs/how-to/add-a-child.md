# Add a child

This guide shows you how to add a child to a family in the hosted app. A family can have up to five
children.

## Add your first child

1. Create the family's account and confirm the email. The app opens "Welcome".
2. Fill in the form as in [Add another child](#add-another-child), step 2.
3. Press "Save and plan the week". The app opens "This week" with the child's card.

If you sign in to a family with no children, "This week" sends you to "Welcome" too.

## Add another child

1. Open "This week" and press "Add a child" under the child cards, or open "Children" and press
   "Add a child". On "This week" the button shows only while the family has fewer than five
   children.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **First name.** Use a first name only.
   - **Interests.** Optional. Separate them with commas, such as `dinosaurs, baking, soccer`.
   - **Math grade** and **Reading and writing grade.** Pick each one for where the child works
     now, not their age. They can differ.
   - **Notes.** Optional. Anything else the planner should know.

   Keep last names, addresses and anything private out of the form. An AI service reads it.
3. Press "Save and plan the week". The app goes back to the page you started from, with the new
   child on it.

The child's first week is planned at once. Which Monday it starts on, and what happens with the
weekly loop on or off: [First week](../reference/children.md#first-week).

## If the form refuses to save

- **"You can add up to five children."** Delete a child first, then add the new one.
- **Too many interests, or one that is too long,** or **"Something in the form needs fixing."**
  Shorten the answer to fit the [limits](../reference/children.md#post-children), then press
  "Save and plan the week" again.
- **"We couldn't save this just now."** Press "Save and plan the week" again.

Fields, limits and pages: [Children reference](../reference/children.md). Why grades are per
subject: [The weekly loop](../explanation/the-weekly-loop.md#why-math-has-its-own-grade).
